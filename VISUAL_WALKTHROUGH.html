🔄 HOW IT ALL WORKS TOGETHER

Here's the complete flow from start to finish:

1️⃣
Customer Visits Portal
They go to /pages/portal.html
They see all your travel packages
⬇️
2️⃣
Customer Searches & Browses
They search by destination
They filter by price and duration
They click "View Details"
⬇️
3️⃣
Customer Fills Booking Form
Their name, email, phone
Departure and return dates
Number of travelers
Any special requests
⬇️
4️⃣
Form Submitted to Airtable
Website sends data to Airtable database
Customer gets confirmation with Booking ID
Status set to "Pending"
⬇️
5️⃣
You Login to Admin Dashboard
Go to /pages/admin-login.html
Enter your email and password
You see all bookings instantly
⬇️
6️⃣
You Manage the Booking
See customer details
Update booking status
Track payment
Send confirmation to customer
⬇️
7️⃣
Everything is Tracked
See all your revenue
View booking statistics
Track which destinations are popular
Monitor payment status
✓ All automatic! No manual data entry - everything flows from the website to Airtable automatically.
